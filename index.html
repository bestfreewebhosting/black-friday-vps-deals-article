Compare renewal prices, not just discounts

A promotional VPS price may apply only to the first billing term. The roundup lists examples such as Hostinger at $6.49/month renewing at $11.99/month, ScalaHosting at $22.46/month renewing at $54.95/month, and Verpex at $10/month renewing at $19.99/month. In contrast, InterServer and some other listed plans are shown with the same renewal price.

Before buying, calculate the total cost for the period you expect to use the server. Include setup fees, control-panel licences, backups, extra IP addresses and any add-ons that are not part of the advertised base plan.

Managed vs unmanaged VPS: what should you choose?

With a managed VPS, the provider takes responsibility for some server administration, such as maintenance, security updates or monitoring. With an unmanaged VPS, you generally have root access but must handle operating-system updates, hardening, backups and troubleshooting yourself.

  • Choose managed if you run a business site and do not have time or experience to maintain a server.
  • Choose unmanaged if you are comfortable with Linux, SSH, firewalls and server maintenance.
  • Check the scope: “managed” can mean different things. Confirm what support actually includes before ordering.

VPS buying checklist

  • Resources: Compare vCPU, RAM and NVMe/SSD storage against your workload.
  • Virtualisation: Check whether the plan uses KVM and whether resources are clearly specified.
  • Renewal: Record the renewal price and the term required to get the advertised rate.
  • Management: Confirm who handles updates, security, monitoring and backups.
  • Location: Choose a data centre close to your visitors where possible.
  • Refund policy: Read the provider's current cancellation and refund terms.
  • Support: Check support channels and whether help with server administration is included.

Frequently asked questions

What is the cheapest Black Friday VPS deal in this comparison?

The roundup lists InterServer from $3/month for its entry plan, with the same renewal price. Check the current checkout page for availability and plan conditions.

Which VPS deal is suitable for users in India?

YouStable is highlighted for India, with a listed starting price of $3.67/month and a Mumbai data-centre option. Compare latency from your target audience's location before deciding.

Are Black Friday VPS prices permanent?

Not always. Some providers renew at a higher rate after the promotional term. Check the renewal price and calculate the full-term cost before paying.

Should I buy managed or unmanaged VPS hosting?

Managed VPS is generally more convenient if you do not want to administer the server. Unmanaged VPS can cost less but requires you to manage server security, updates and troubleshooting.

Are Cyber Monday VPS deals better than Black Friday deals?

Many providers run one promotion across both dates. Compare the actual checkout price and offer terms rather than assuming Cyber Monday will be cheaper.
